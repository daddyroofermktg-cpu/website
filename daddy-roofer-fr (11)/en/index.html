FAQ

Your questions, answered

Materials, maintenance, inspections, emergencies: answers to the questions we hear most often.

Materials

What is the difference between a TPO membrane and a gravel roof?

A TPO membrane is a thermoplastic sheet installed on a flat roof, with heat-welded seams. It is often chosen for commercial buildings. A gravel roof is a more traditional multi-layer system. During the inspection we explain which one suits your building best.

Which materials are used for a pitched roof?

It depends on the slope, the budget and the style of the house: asphalt shingles, metal and other options. We walk you through the choices during the visit.

Maintenance and inspections

How often should a roof be inspected?

One inspection a year is a good habit, and it is worth having one after a major storm or hail.

What are the signs that work is needed?

Leaks or stains on the ceiling, lifted or missing shingles, cracks or blisters on a membrane, water pooling on a flat roof.

Emergencies and damage

What should I do if my roof leaks?

Protect the inside (move belongings, place a container), take photos and contact us quickly. Avoid climbing on the roof yourself. Also let your insurer know.
