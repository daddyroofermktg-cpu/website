Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

DR Toiture & Construction (“Daddy Roofer”, “we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect on this site, why, and how we protect it.

Information collected

When you fill in a form, we collect the information you provide: name, phone number, postal code, property and project type, details of your request and, if you wish, a photo of your roof.

Use

We use this information only to answer your request, schedule a visit and prepare a quote. We do not sell it.

Transmission and retention

Requests are sent to our team by email and stored by our form hosting provider (Netlify). We keep your information for as long as needed to handle your request and meet our legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to contact@daddyroofer.com.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. If audience measurement tools are added, this policy will be updated.
