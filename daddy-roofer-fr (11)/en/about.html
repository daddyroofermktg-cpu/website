About

A nickname earned on the job site.

Daddy Roofer is a residential and commercial roofing company established in Quebec, built on more than 28 years of hands-on experience, technical mastery paired with a deep understanding of what every client deserves: clarity, honesty, and work done right the first time.

The name "Daddy Roofer" was born on job sites, a nickname earned through leadership, experience, and a culture of sharing knowledge. We don't just build roofs. We build trust.
